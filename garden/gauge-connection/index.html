Gauge connection

seedling · planted 27 November 2026 #geometry

A connection on a principal 𝐺-bundle is a Lie-algebra valued one-form 𝐴. Under a gauge transformation 𝑔(𝑥) it transforms as

𝐴→𝑔𝐴𝑔−1+𝑔d𝑔−1

and its curvature is 𝐹=d𝐴+𝐴∧𝐴. Parallel transport around a closed loop gives the Wilson loop for a 𝑈(1) bundle over parameter space it is the Berry phase.
